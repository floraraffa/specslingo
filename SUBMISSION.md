## Project description

Lingo Specs is an AI-powered spatial language coach for SPECS. It guides learners from vocabulary to real-world communication through two complementary modes. AI Image Cards provide consistent illustrated vocabulary that users can listen to, pronounce, translate, and organize into six contextual collections. Scan My World uses the SPECS camera and OpenAI to understand the current setting, label multiple visible objects, and generate useful phrases the learner can immediately listen to and practice—for example, ordering a drink in a café. Objects found during scanning become persistent practice cards for later sessions.

The experience supports Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, and Japanese. Feedback, explanations, pronunciation coaching, and mascot artwork adapt to the learner's native language. SPECS ASR and OpenAI provide phonetic evaluation and teaching guidance, while audio/text XP and persistent progress encourage repeated practice.

Lingo Specs was designed for travelers, language learners, and anyone who needs help turning vocabulary into confident communication in the moment.

## How it uses CLAD

CLAD was used throughout the complete closed loop: project validation, UIKit construction, TypeScript authoring, AI/camera/ASR integration, compilation, runtime-log analysis, preview inspection, debugging, localization, visual refinement, persistence, and final delivery. The human creator supplied the concept, original artwork, and iterative visual direction; the AI co-developer implemented and verified the Lens directly inside Lens Studio.
