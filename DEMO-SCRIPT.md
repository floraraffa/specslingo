# Lingo Specs — Demo Video Script

Lenslist requests a walkthrough of the spatial experience, its key features, and how CLAD was used. Target length: **75–100 seconds**.

## Recording plan

### 0–8s — Title

- Show the Lingo Specs logo in the SPECS preview.
- Voice-over: “Lingo Specs is an AI spatial language coach built for SPECS with CLAD.”

### 8–20s — Personalized setup

- Choose a native language.
- Show the country mascot, localized greeting, and user name.
- Choose a target language.
- Voice-over: “The whole experience adapts to the learner's native language.”

### 20–42s — AI Image Cards

- Select AI Image Cards.
- Show a kawaii vocabulary card.
- Press Listen, then Hold to Talk.
- Show correct or coaching feedback and the XP update.
- Drag or place the card into its contextual collection.

### 42–72s — Scan My World

- Return and select Scan My World.
- Scan a café, desk, kitchen, or living-room scene containing several clear objects.
- Show the detected setting and multiple object labels.
- Select a practical situation phrase, listen, and practice it.
- Show that detected objects were saved as cards.

### 72–88s — Persistence and usefulness

- Open saved AI Image Cards and show a previously scanned object.
- Show retained XP/progress.
- Voice-over: “What you discover in the real world becomes a personalized practice library for later.”

### 88–100s — CLAD close

- Briefly show Lens Studio with a successful TypeScript compile or the CLAD log.
- Voice-over: “CLAD helped build, test, debug, and refine the entire Lens in a closed loop.”
- End on the logo and Week 2: Guide.

## Recording checklist

- Use a high-contrast environment with 3–5 recognizable objects.
- Record one successful pronunciation and one coaching response.
- Keep the full rounded panel inside frame.
- Avoid displaying the Remote Service Gateway token panel or any credentials.
- Upload the final video to Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer with public link access.
- Paste the link into `SUBMISSION.md` and the Lenslist submission form.
